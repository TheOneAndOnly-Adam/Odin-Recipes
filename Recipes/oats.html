Overnight Oats

Overnight oats are a quick and easy breakfast option that can be prepared the night before. They are typically made with rolled oats, milk, and a sweetener, and can be topped with fruits, nuts, or seeds.

They are a great option for busy mornings, as they can be eaten cold or warmed up, and are highly customizable to suit different tastes and dietary needs.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk (dairy or plant-based)
  • 1 tablespoon sweetener (honey, maple syrup, or sugar)
  • Optional toppings: fruits, nuts, seeds, nut butter

Instructions

  1. Combine the oats, milk, and sweetener in a bowl or jar.
  2. Stir well to ensure the oats are evenly coated.
  3. Cover the bowl or jar and refrigerate overnight.
  4. In the morning, add your desired toppings and enjoy cold or warmed up.

Enjoy your delicious and nutritious overnight oats!
