Spaghetti

Spaghetti is a classic Italian dish made with long, thin pasta and a variety of sauces. It is versatile and can be customized with different ingredients and toppings.

Whether you prefer a simple tomato sauce, creamy carbonara, or hearty meat sauce, spaghetti is a comforting and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Tomato sauce or your preferred sauce
  • Optional toppings: grated cheese, fresh herbs, meatballs, vegetables

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente.
  2. Drain the pasta and set aside.
  3. Prepare your chosen sauce in a separate pan.
  4. Combine the cooked spaghetti with the sauce and toss gently to coat.
  5. Serve hot with your desired toppings.

Enjoy your delicious and satisfying spaghetti!
